What changed since 2010

  • DAT was replaced by DPU: the place of delivery need not be a terminal
  • CIP requires extended ICC (A) insurance cover
  • Under FCA the parties can agree on an on-board bill of lading
  • Seller and buyer may carry the goods with their own transport
