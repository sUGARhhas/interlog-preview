How we work

One forwarder for the whole journey: from request to destination station.

  1. Request

    You describe the cargo, volume and route by phone, email or the form on this site.

  2. Quote

    We calculate the rail tariff and select wagons and a delivery plan.

  3. Paperwork

    We prepare shipping documents, pay the tariff and handle customs.

  4. Shipment

    We track the wagons and border crossings all the way to the consignee.
